# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 11, 2026

## Privacy Policy for Cipher

Cipher is a Figma plugin focused on importing, exporting, and synchronizing design tokens.

This Privacy Policy explains how Cipher handles information in its current beta version.

## 1. Overview

Cipher is currently a **local-only beta plugin**. At this stage, Cipher does **not require user accounts** and is designed to operate within Figma without transmitting user content to external servers controlled by Cipher.

## 2. Information We Collect

At this time, Cipher does **not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information** through the plugin.

In its current beta version, Cipher does not require users to create an account or submit personal profile information in order to use the plugin.

## 3. Design Data and Token Data

Cipher is designed to work with design tokens and related system data inside the Figma environment.

At this time, Cipher does **not intentionally transmit token data, design data, or file contents to external servers** operated by Cipher.

## 4. Analytics and Tracking

Cipher does **not currently use analytics, behavioral tracking, or telemetry inside the plugin**.

If analytics or usage tracking are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 5. Cookies and Website Data

If you visit the Cipher website, limited technical information may be collected automatically by your browser or hosting provider, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and standard log data.

This information is used only for basic website delivery, security, and maintenance, and is not used by Cipher to identify individual users personally.

If additional analytics, cookies, email forms, or third-party services are added to the website in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

## 6. Data Sharing

Cipher does **not sell personal data**.

Because Cipher does not currently collect personal data through the plugin, it does not intentionally share such data with third parties.

## 7. Data Retention

Cipher does not currently maintain user accounts or host user data on external servers. As a result, Cipher does not currently retain personal user account data through the plugin.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Cipher is not directed to children under 13, and it is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as Cipher evolves.

If the plugin later adds features such as accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or third-party integrations, this policy will be revised to reflect those changes.
